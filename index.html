Hiking, archery, and camping. Explore the outdoors and try something new with the pack.

A few things to know

Who can join Pack 12?

Pack 12 is for girls and boys in grades K–5. “A Pack for All People” is the welcome shared on our original website.

When and where do you meet?

September–May meetings are the first three Tuesdays of the month at 6:30 pm at Yates Baptist Church, 2819 Chapel Hill Rd. in Durham, next to Rogers-Herr Middle School. Contact the pack to confirm a visit.

Where do I find registration details?

Read the original registration guide (PDF, English and Spanish). Because the document is dated 2024, contact the pack to confirm current costs and instructions.
